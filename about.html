About Us

Christmas Cards Hub is a small, passionate project that helps people create, personalize, and share animated Christmas cards. Our mission is to make festive greetings easy, delightful, and accessible to everyone —

We focus on simple tools, charming designs, and respectful defaults. You can customize card text, toggle characters, adjust positions, and export animations as GIFs or WebM files. If you have feedback or would like to contribute, please reach out via the contact form or check our project README.
